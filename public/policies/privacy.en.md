# EN — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Atlan Performance (“Atlan”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information when you visit our website and PWA (the “Platform”) or contact us.

#### 1. Who we are
Atlan Performance is a sports coaching service (swimming, triathlon, and related programs) based in Costa Rica.

**Contact:**
- Email: erbonilla@outlook.com

#### 2. Information we collect
**A) Information you provide**
- **Contact/trial form:** name, email, phone (optional), experience level, program interest (optional), message (optional), and confirmation that you accept this policy.
- **Newsletter (if available):** email and optional name.

**B) Automatically collected information**
- Basic technical and usage data (e.g., device type, browser, pages visited, interactions).
- Preferences saved in your browser (e.g., language and theme) via cookies.

**C) What we don’t ask for**
- We do not request sensitive data (e.g., medical information) through the Platform. If you voluntarily share it in a message, we’ll handle it carefully and only to support your request.

#### 3. How we use your information
We use information to:
- Reply to inquiries and coordinate a **trial session**.
- Understand program interest and improve the Platform.
- Send requested communications (e.g., newsletter, if you subscribe).
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.

#### 4. Legal basis
Depending on the situation, we process data because:
- You provide **consent** (e.g., submitting a form and accepting this policy).
- It’s necessary to **respond to your request** (e.g., scheduling a trial).
- We have a **legitimate interest** in improving and protecting the Platform.

#### 5. Sharing with service providers
We may share information with vendors that help us run the Platform, such as:
- Analytics providers (to understand Platform usage).
- Map providers (to show locations).
- Infrastructure providers (hosting, CDN) and/or email/API services for form delivery.

We share only what’s necessary for the specific purpose.

#### 6. International transfers
Some providers may process data outside Costa Rica. When this happens, we aim to use providers with reasonable security measures and data protection practices.

#### 7. Retention
We keep your data only as long as needed to:
- Respond to your request.
- Maintain reasonable operational records (e.g., lead follow-up).
- Comply with legal obligations, where applicable.

#### 8. Your rights
You can request:
- Access to your data.
- Correction of inaccurate data.
- Deletion (where applicable).
- Withdrawal of consent (where applicable).

To exercise these rights, contact **erbonilla@outlook.com**.

#### 9. Children
The Platform isn’t intended for children without supervision. If a minor wants to train, a parent/guardian authorization and involvement may be required.

#### 10. Security
We use reasonable safeguards (access controls, technical best practices). However, no system is 100% secure.

#### 11. Changes
We may update this policy. We’ll publish the current version on the Platform with an updated date.
