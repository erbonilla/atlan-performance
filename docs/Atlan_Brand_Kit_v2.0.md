## 0. Governance & Maintenance

### 0.1 Document Purpose

This Brand Kit serves as the single source of truth for the Atlan Performance brand identity. It ensures consistency across all touchpoints—digital, physical, and interpersonal—while providing flexibility for creative execution within defined boundaries.

### 0.2 The Atlan Standard (Compliance Hierarchy)

| Level | Elements | Flexibility |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **LOCKED** (Mandatory) | Logo geometry, Primary color palette (Abyss Navy, Tide Teal, Coral Spark, Foam), Core value definitions, WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards, Wordmark typography (Outfit) | None—never alter |
| **FLEXIBLE** (Creative) | Photography selection (within style guide), Layout variations (within grid system), Motion transition speeds, Secondary color applications | Within guidelines |
| **PROHIBITED** | Distorting logo, Non-approved typefaces for data, Shaming or purely authoritarian tone, Using Tide Teal for small body text | Never permitted |

## 2. Market Positioning & Messaging

### 2.1 Audience Segmentation (Psychographic)

We target based on **mindset**, not demographics:

| Persona | Core Drive | Pain Point | Key Need |
|---------|------------|------------|----------|
| **The Optimizer** | Efficiency above all | Time scarcity, guilt about training vs. family | "Minimum Effective Dose" to get results |
| **The Seeker** | Curiosity, pushing limits | Information overload, conflicting advice | Data, nuance, the "why" behind training |

### 2.2 High-Value Personas

#### Persona 1: The Competitive Triathlete ("The Seeker")

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Demographics** | 30-50 years old, professional career, disposable income |
| **Core Desire** | Self-mastery—seeing exactly how far their body can go |
| **Behavior** | Researches extensively, compares data, values expertise |
| **Messaging** | "Scientific protocols," "Unlock your physiological limit," "Precision engineering" |
| **Objections** | "Is this just another generic training plan?" |

#### Persona 2: The Weekend Warrior ("The Optimizer")

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Demographics** | 35-55 years old, career/family balance, limited training windows |
| **Core Pain Point** | Time scarcity & guilt—fears training steals from family or life derails training |
| **Behavior** | Seeks efficiency, values clear ROI on time invested |
| **Messaging** | "High-ROI training," "Life-Sport Balance," "Make every minute count" |
| **Objections** | "I don't have time for a serious program" |

### 2.3 Strategic Differentiators

#### Technical Mastery Protocol (The Swim Anchor)

We prioritize technical development using this hierarchy:

```
1. BREATHING → Foundation of efficiency
       ↓
2. BODY POSITION → Reduces drag, enables power
       ↓
3. CATCH → Maximizes propulsion per stroke
```

**Outcome:** Turn the athlete's weakest discipline into a strength.

#### Holistic Longevity

We integrate targeted mobility and activation work into every program:

| Element | Purpose | Frequency |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Pre-swim activation | Prepare joints and muscles | Every session |
| Post-workout mobility | Accelerate recovery | Every session |
| Injury prevention protocols | Address common triathlete issues | Weekly |

**Outcome:** Athletes can keep racing for decades, not just seasons.

| **The Problem** | What stands in their way | Stuck at a plateau or overwhelmed by conflicting advice |

| **The Success** | What the hero achieves | Reaching the finish line strong, injury-free, and proud |

## 4. Verbal Identity & Tone

### 4.1 Voice Character

**The Mix:** Analytic + Motivational

We acknowledge the data (reality) first, then provide the encouragement (spirit) to improve it.

| Voice Attribute | Expression |
|-----------------|------------|
| **Expert** | Speak with authority backed by data |
| **Supportive** | Acknowledge struggles without shaming |
| **Clear** | Use accessible language, avoid jargon |
| **Motivating** | End with actionable encouragement |

### 4.2 Tone Spectrum

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| **Data Feedback** | Analytical, precise | "Your swim pace improved 8% this week—2:15/100m vs. 2:27 baseline." |
| **Encouragement** | Warm, supportive | "The data shows you're ready to add threshold work. Let's push." |
| **Correction** | Firm but kind | "Your catch is slipping late in sets. Focus on early vertical forearm." |
| **Celebration** | Proud, team-oriented | "Relentless forward motion. You earned this." |
| **Recovery** | Empathetic, non-judgmental | "Life happens—the important thing is you're back today." |

### 4.3 Voice Examples

#### ✅ Good Examples (Sage + Hero Balance)

**Workout Recap:**
> **ES:** "Tu ritmo de nado fue 2:15/100m la semana pasada—8% más rápido que tu línea base. Los datos muestran que estás listo para agregar trabajo de umbral. Vamos a exigirte."
>
> **EN:** "Your swim pace was 2:15/100m last week—8% faster than your baseline. The data shows you're ready to add threshold work. Let's push."

**Missed Session:**
> **ES:** "Veo que saltaste el entrenamiento del miércoles. La vida pasa—lo importante es que vuelvas hoy. Tu próxima sesión: técnica de brazada, 45 min."
>
> **EN:** "I see you missed Wednesday's session. Life happens—the important thing is you're back today. Your next session: stroke technique, 45 min."

#### ❌ Bad Examples (What to Avoid)

**Pure Hero (No Sage):**
> "¡Lo aplastaste! ¡Sigue moliendo! ¡Sin dolor no hay ganancia!"

*Why it fails:* No data, purely motivational fluff—violates Sage primacy.

**Pure Sage (No Hero):**
> "Tu umbral de lactato indica una capacidad anaeróbica subóptima relativa a tu VO2max proyectado."

*Why it fails:* Too clinical, no encouragement—violates accessibility.

**Shaming:**
> "Otra sesión perdida. ¿Cuándo te vas a tomar esto en serio?"

*Why it fails:* Violates athlete-centric value, creates guilt—expressly prohibited.

### 4.5 Writing Guidelines

| Guideline | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Lead with data** | Start feedback with specific metrics before encouragement |
| **Use active voice** | "You improved 8%" not "An improvement of 8% was observed" |
| **Be specific** | "Focus on early vertical forearm" not "Work on your technique" |
| **Acknowledge context** | "Life happens" validates athlete's real-world constraints |
| **End with action** | Always provide next step or encouragement |

## 5. Digital Experience Standards

### 5.1 Motion Strategy: "Liquid Precision"

The motion language mirrors the brand philosophy—combining organic fluidity with mechanical precision.

#### The Two Layers

| Layer | Character | Application | Timing |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Liquid** | Smooth, flowing, organic | Hero images, backgrounds, page transitions, navigation | 0.6s ease-out |
| **Precision** | Snappy, mechanical, sharp | Metrics, counters, form validation, data updates | 0.2s ease |

#### CSS Implementation

```css
:root {
  /* Liquid: Brand/story elements */
  --transition-liquid: all 0.6s cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94);
  
  /* Precision: Data/UI elements */
  --transition-snap: all 0.2s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1);
  
  /* Hover: Quick feedback */
  --transition-hover: transform 0.3s ease-out;
  
  /* Page: Route transitions */
  --transition-page: opacity 0.4s ease-in-out;
}
```

#### Application Guide

| Element | Transition | Rationale |
|---------|------------|-----------|
| Hero image fade | Liquid | Evokes water's natural flow |
| Navigation slide | Liquid | Organic, welcoming movement |
| Card hover lift | Hover | Quick but not jarring feedback |
| Button click | Precision | Immediate, responsive feedback |
| Form error | Precision | Snappy, attention-grabbing |
| Metric counter | Precision | Like a stopwatch—mechanical |
| Page reveal | Liquid | Smooth, professional transition |

### 5.2 Data Visualization Standards

#### Default Interface: Dark Mode

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Background** | Abyss Navy (#0B2A3C) |
| **Grid lines** | Foam at 10% opacity |
| **Primary data line** | Tide Teal (#0E8A9A), 2px solid |
| **Threshold/alert data** | Coral Spark (#FF6A3D), 2px dashed |
| **Labels** | Roboto Mono, Foam (#ECF7F8) |
| **Axis values** | Lato, Foam (#ECF7F8), 12px |

#### The "Color Blind" Protocol

**Mandate:** Color alone is never sufficient to distinguish data points.

| Visual Differentiation | Example |
|------------------------|---------|
| Color + Pattern | Solid Teal line vs. Dashed Coral line |
| Color + Shape | Teal circles vs. Coral squares |
| Color + Label | Direct data labels on each line |

```css

  stroke-width: 2px;
  stroke-dasharray: none; /* Solid */
}

  stroke-width: 2px;
  stroke-dasharray: 8, 4; /* Dashed */
}
```

## 7. Accessibility & Inclusion

### 7.1 Mandatory Accessibility Standards

**Target:** WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance for all digital products.

| Requirement | Standard | Implementation |
|-------------|----------|----------------|
| Color contrast (normal text) | 4.5:1 minimum | Use approved color pairings only |
| Color contrast (large text) | 3:1 minimum | 18px+ bold or 24px+ regular |
| Touch targets | 48px × 48px minimum | All interactive elements |
| Keyboard navigation | Full support | Tab order, focus states |
| Screen reader support | Semantic HTML + ARIA | Labels, roles, live regions |
| Reduced motion | Respect preference | `@prefers-reduced-motion` |

### 7.2 Data Visualization Accessibility

**Rule:** Charts must never rely on color alone to convey meaning.

| Method | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Pattern + Color | Solid line vs. dashed line |
| Shape + Color | Circle markers vs. square markers |
| Label + Color | Direct labels on each data series |
| Texture + Color | Filled vs. hatched chart areas |

### 7.3 Inclusive Design

#### Representation

Brand imagery must reflect diversity in:
- Age (athletes from 20s to 60s)
- Body type (various athletic builds)
- Adaptive ability (where appropriate)
- Ethnicity (reflective of Costa Rica's diversity)

#### Language

| Guideline | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| **Person-first language** | "Athlete with an injury" not "Injured athlete" |
| **Ability-neutral** | "Uses adaptive equipment" not "Handicapped" |
| **Gender-inclusive** | "Athletes" not "Guys" |
| **Non-shaming** | "Missed session" not "Failed to show up" |

### 7.4 Reduced Motion Support

```css
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  *,
  *::before,
  *::after {
    animation-duration: 0.01ms !important;
    animation-iteration-count: 1 !important;
    transition-duration: 0.01ms !important;
    scroll-behavior: auto !important;
  }
}
```

### 8.1 Strategic Partnership Framework

#### The "Host" Hierarchy

In all co-branding scenarios, Atlan Performance retains visual lead.

| Rule | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| **Size** | Atlan wordmark at least 25% larger than partner logo |
| **Position** | Atlan logo positioned above or to the left of partner |

| **Clear space** | No partner elements within Atlan logo clear space |
